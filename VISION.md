
# Vision

A **companion web app** for learning to play the Indian flute (Bansuri).
The app is not a static tutorial but an **interactive musical partner** — it listens, responds, and gently guides.

---

## Core Intention

The app acts like a **companion flute player**:

* It listens to what I play on my bansuri.
* It plays back my notes with **subtle modifications** — sometimes correcting, sometimes extending.
* It gradually introduces **beautiful tunes and variations**, helping me discover the **full spectrum of bansuri playing**.

Learning happens through **playful musical dialogue**, rather than rigid lessons. The app evolves with me, becoming both **teacher and duet partner**.

---

## Principles

1. **Companionship over instruction**
   The app should feel like a friend or mentor playing alongside me, not a strict teacher.

2. **Exploration through sound**
   Learning comes from hearing, imitating, and improvising — not from reading notation or rigid theory.

3. **Progressive introduction of beauty**
   Over time, the app should expose me to **ragas, ornaments, and phrasing** in Indian flute music, making learning organic and joyful.

4. **Adaptive responsiveness**
   The app must always react to my playing in a way that feels alive, musical, and supportive.

---

## Constraints

* ❌ **No rigid scoring systems** (avoid “gamification” that distracts from musical flow).
* ❌ **No Western-style sheet music learning** — focus on **listening, imitation, and improvisation** instead.
* ❌ **No overwhelming theory upfront** — concepts of raga, tala, or ornamentation should emerge gradually and naturally.
* ❌ **No robotic playback** — responses must always feel human-like and musically alive.
* ✅ Keep it **lightweight, joyful, and playful**.
* ✅ Prioritize **immersion in sound and feel** over analytics or metrics.

---

## Desired Outcomes

* I feel more confident playing bansuri.
* I naturally absorb new tunes and styles without forced practice.
* Playing with the app feels **fun, alive, and musically rewarding**.
* Over time, I grow closer to the **soul of Indian flute music**.
